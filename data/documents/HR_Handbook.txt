NovaTech Employee Handbook — 2025

NovaTech provides 20 days of annual paid time off for full-time employees, in addition to local public holidays and statutory leave. Employees submit planned leave through the HR portal and coordinate coverage with their manager.

The company supports inclusive hiring, equal opportunity, and reasonable workplace accommodations. Retaliation for raising a good-faith concern is prohibited. Employees may contact People Operations or the confidential ethics channel.

Performance conversations take place twice each year. Learning budgets are approved by managers within team allocations. Employee records and compensation information are confidential and are accessed only by authorized People Operations staff.

Remote-work arrangements follow the Remote Work Policy. Company devices must use multi-factor authentication and approved software. Employees should complete annual security and privacy training.
