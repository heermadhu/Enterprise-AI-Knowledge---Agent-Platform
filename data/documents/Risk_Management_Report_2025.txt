NovaTech Risk Management Report — 2025

Information security risks include credential theft, misconfigured cloud storage, third-party service outages, and unauthorized access to customer data. The September NovaSecure authentication rollout increased administrator verification steps; customer onboarding friction and support volume should be monitored while the control remains in place.

Business risks include customer concentration in large enterprise contracts, delayed customer acceptance that shifts revenue between months, and reliance on a small number of cloud infrastructure suppliers. Revenue timing should not be confused with lost annual contract value.

Mitigations include multi-factor authentication, least-privilege access, vendor continuity reviews, incident exercises, documented change management, and customer acceptance tracking. Security exceptions require a named owner and expiration date.

The report identifies risks and controls but does not quantify probability or financial loss for each risk.
