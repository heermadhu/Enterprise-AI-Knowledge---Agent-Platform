NovaTech Annual Business Review — 2025

NovaTech's 2025 monthly recognized revenue was January $420,000, February $430,000, March $445,000, April $450,000, May $462,000, June $480,000, July $500,000, August $525,000, September $472,000, October $535,000, November $550,000, and December $585,000. Full-year recognized revenue was $5,854,000.

Quarter totals were Q1 $1,295,000, Q2 $1,392,000, Q3 $1,497,000, and Q4 $1,670,000. Each quarter total equals its three monthly revenue entries.

NovaCloud generated the highest product revenue over the year. September revenue declined from August; the Q3 report records a $53,000 decrease and describes several product-level movements, onboarding friction after a NovaSecure authentication change, and a $22,000 customer acceptance delay. These observations should not be treated as definitive causal proof.

Revenue is recognized in the month of the order in the operating dataset. Financial figures exclude taxes and refunds.
