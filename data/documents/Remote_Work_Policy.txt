NovaTech Remote Work Policy

Effective date: January 1, 2025. Owner: People Operations.

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week with manager approval. Teams set core collaboration hours between 10:00 and 15:00 in their primary time zone. New hires should spend their first two weeks onsite or in an approved team onboarding location when practical.

Employees must use company-managed devices, multi-factor authentication, and the approved secure access service when connecting to NovaTech systems. Confidential customer data must not be downloaded to personal devices or shared through unapproved file-storage services. Report a lost device or suspected disclosure to Security within one hour.

Managers review team coverage quarterly. Exceptions for accessibility, caregiving, or approved accommodations are handled confidentially with People Operations. This policy does not change local employment terms.
