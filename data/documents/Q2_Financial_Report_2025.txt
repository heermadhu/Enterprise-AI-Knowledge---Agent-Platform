NovaTech Q2 Financial Report — 2025

Reporting period: April through June 2025. All values are USD.

Monthly revenue was April $450,000, May $462,000, and June $480,000. Q2 recognized revenue totaled $1,392,000, an increase of $97,000 (7.5%) compared with Q1.

Product revenue for Q2: NovaCloud $486,000; NovaSecure $301,000; NovaAnalytics $249,000; NovaFlow $196,000; NovaAI $160,000. The product figures sum to reported Q2 revenue.

The quarter included 300 completed customer orders in the operating dataset. NovaCloud remained the largest product by recognized revenue. Revenue is recognized in the month the order is recorded; figures exclude taxes and refunds.
