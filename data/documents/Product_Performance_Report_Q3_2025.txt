NovaTech Product Performance Report — Q3 2025

September recognized revenue was $472,000, down $53,000 (10.1%) from August's $525,000. These amounts agree with the monthly business ledger.

The month-over-month product changes were: NovaCloud decreased $14,000 ($180,000 to $166,000); NovaSecure decreased $31,000 ($115,000 to $84,000); NovaAnalytics decreased $5,000 ($90,000 to $85,000); NovaFlow decreased $8,000 ($75,000 to $67,000); and NovaAI increased $5,000 ($65,000 to $70,000). Net change: negative $53,000.

NovaSecure's decline coincided with the September authentication policy rollout. Implementation teams reported extra administrator verification, slower onboarding, and increased support contacts. Finance noted a $22,000 enterprise order moved from September recognition to October after customer acceptance testing was delayed. The report does not establish that the rollout caused all of the product decline; timing and customer acceptance are contributing observations.

NovaCloud's decrease reflected two enterprise expansions that moved into October. NovaFlow had fewer large workflow expansions. NovaAI grew due to three new team deployments. This report describes observed timing and support signals; it does not claim a complete causal attribution.
