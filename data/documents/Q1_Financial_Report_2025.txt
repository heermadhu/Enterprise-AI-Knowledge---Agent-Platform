NovaTech Q1 Financial Report — 2025

Reporting period: January through March 2025. All values are USD.

Monthly revenue was January $420,000, February $430,000, and March $445,000. Q1 recognized revenue totaled $1,295,000. Revenue grew steadily as enterprise adoption of NovaCloud and NovaSecure expanded.

Product revenue for Q1: NovaCloud $450,000; NovaSecure $279,000; NovaAnalytics $232,000; NovaFlow $184,000; NovaAI $150,000. The product figures sum to reported Q1 revenue.

The quarter closed with 300 completed customer orders in the operating dataset. Revenue is recognized in the month the order is recorded. Figures exclude taxes and refunds.
