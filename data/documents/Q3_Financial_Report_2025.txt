NovaTech Q3 Financial Report — 2025

Reporting period: July through September 2025. All values are USD.

Monthly revenue was July $500,000, August $525,000, and September $472,000. Q3 recognized revenue totaled $1,497,000, an increase of $105,000 (7.5%) compared with Q2. September revenue fell $53,000 (10.1%) from August.

Product revenue for Q3: NovaCloud $520,000; NovaSecure $307,000; NovaAnalytics $261,000; NovaFlow $214,000; NovaAI $195,000. The product figures sum to reported Q3 revenue.

September recognized revenue by product was NovaCloud $166,000, NovaSecure $84,000, NovaAnalytics $85,000, NovaFlow $67,000, and NovaAI $70,000. Product revenue in August was respectively $180,000, $115,000, $90,000, $75,000, and $65,000.

The quarter included 300 completed customer orders in the operating dataset. The September product mix reflects contracted order revenue recorded during the month, not a measure of annual recurring revenue.
