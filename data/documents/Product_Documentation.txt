NovaTech Product Documentation — Product overview

NovaCloud provides managed cloud infrastructure, backup, and elastic compute for enterprise workloads. Its standard enterprise subscription starts at $129 per workload per month; negotiated contracts may differ.

NovaSecure provides identity governance, threat monitoring, and endpoint protection. The standard subscription starts at $89 per protected user per month. The September 2025 authentication policy rollout required additional administrator verification. Customer implementation teams reported longer onboarding and elevated support contacts after that rollout.

NovaAnalytics provides governed dashboards and forecasting, starting at $99 per analyst per month. NovaFlow automates approvals and cross-system workflows, starting at $79 per workflow per month. NovaAI provides governed generative AI assistants with tenant isolation and retrieval controls, starting at $149 per seat per month.

List prices describe standard subscriptions and are not the same as recognized revenue in the financial reports. Enterprise discounts and order timing affect reported monthly revenue.
